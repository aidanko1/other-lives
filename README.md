# Other Lives v5.3 — Age and everyday-life audit

Play: https://aidanko1.github.io/other-lives/ . This is an independent, fictional text-led life simulator. GitHub Pages hosts the web app; internet is required.

## Changes in v5.3
- Children under 12 do not manage a personal budget, buy things, take loans, invest, or pay family bills. Family finances remain tracked behind the scenes.
- Teenagers aged 12–17 receive a separate, limited allowance (when affordable), with appropriate free and low-cost activities. It does not give access to parents' cash.
- Childhood medical and educational matters use age-appropriate scenarios: caregivers arrange vaccinations, schooling and treatment while children may communicate their needs.
- Training or higher-education qualifications are awarded at completion, not immediately on application/enrolment.
- Ordinary journal entries call parents Mom/Dad without bracketed names, while Family/People profiles keep their identifying names.
- Culture appears in meals, transport, errands, schooling and family routines, not only annual festivals. There are 79 illustrative country-specific food pools with varied, non-universal scenes for other locations.
- The four status indicators are more compact; playback controls do not overlap; navigation is above the life story rather than floating over the content.
- Birth journal entries are not automatically marked as negative because the family experiences hardship.

## Tests
129 country/region life simulations completed with no game exceptions. Age-dependent spending, decision windows, cause-of-death records, journal naming, and cultural event availability were checked. Browser widths of 320, 375, 390, 430, 768 and 1024 pixels showed no horizontal scrolling. The set-aside decision returned to the same unanswered choice.

## Limits and saving
Country-specific mortality, income, jobs, costs, and cultural participation are still **fictional approximations**, not scientifically verified distributions. A food example is never intended to imply that everyone in the country eats it. This was not tested on a physical iPhone. Existing saves remain stored in your browser; export a JSON backup before clearing data, reinstalling the app, or moving to a new website.
