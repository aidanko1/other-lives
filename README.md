# Other Lives — v3.0
An independent, illustrated, **text-first life simulator** about circumstance, agency and chance. Inspired by the life-simulator genre; **not affiliated with or a reproduction of the code, art, text or proprietary data of 1 in 8 Billion**.

## Play on iPhone
Visit **https://aidanko1.github.io/other-lives/** in **Safari**. To install: Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App.

## What's in this release
- Continuous automatic life progression with speed control, varied narrative entries and occasional pausing decision cards
- Set aside a decision without losing it; opening Profile, Family, People, Facts or **Spend & invest** pauses the timeline
- Spend cash on essentials, care, books, training, transport, gifts, leisure and possessions; separately manage debt, investments, housing and eligible property purchases
- Spending produces persistent transaction records, alters character statistics and can change living conditions, future events and memories
- Country-informed names, schooling, jobs, family finances and situations; richer local story packs for **12** selected countries and regions
- Named mother and father, evolving circumstances of family and friends, remembered pets and teachers, and illustrative contemporaries living in other countries
- A staged randomised birth screen, a dedicated death record with cause and explanatory context, and an archive of prior lives
- Compact iPhone playback controls, non-overlapping tabs and original overhead pixel-art illustrations

## Important limits
**This is a substantial playable simulation, not a validated representation of the global population.** Its 57 locations do **not** have equal narrative depth. Only a subset of under-five mortality estimates uses 2024 World Bank/UN IGME data. Adult death risks, household multipliers, income and career outcomes, socioeconomic mobility, country weighting and spending prices are **illustrative, hand-tuned gameplay assumptions**, not measured individual probabilities or verified national distributions.

Factual tooltips are labelled as population statistics and include sources where relevant; they should not be interpreted as the probability that the character will experience the displayed situation.

## Saves
Local saves stay on your device. **Export your Life Archive as a JSON backup before upgrading**, switching website domains or reinstalling. The GitHub repository and Pages site are public; your in-game saves are not uploaded by the game.
