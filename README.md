# Other Lives — v3

An independent, illustrated text-first life simulator about **circumstances, choices and chance**.

## Play on iPhone
Open https://aidanko1.github.io/other-lives/ in **Safari**, then Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App. Changes are published via GitHub Pages Actions.

## What's new
- Auto-progressing life timeline with multiple narrative events per year and consequential decision interruptions
- Set aside a decision, inspect Family / Money / People / Facts, and return without losing the choice
- Birth roulette and a dedicated death screen with recorded age and fictional cause
- Better relationships, parents' roles, family circumstances, interests, personal memories
- 12 expanded country story packs with named local work offers
- Partial evidence calibration: UNICEF 2024 under-five mortality for 11 countries, approximate World Bank 2024 life-expectancy anchors for 12
- Family archive, replay points, and local JSON backups
- Better mobile layout, controls and pixel-art identity

## Data honesty
There are 57 selectable locations, **but only 12 have expanded local narratives**. Economic outcomes, life tables for most countries, risk multipliers and many events remain model estimates. Read [research and limitations](RESEARCH_AND_LIMITATIONS.md) before treating results as facts. The game's factual tooltips are descriptive population statistics and **not causal or personalised risk calculations**.

## Privacy and saving
The repository/site are publicly accessible. Personal simulated lives are saved in browser storage, not shared to GitHub. Export your Life Archive before changing addresses, upgrading or reinstalling.
