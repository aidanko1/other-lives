# 1 in 8 Billion: verified routes and systems

Research snapshot: 20 October 2026, official English game, v0.7.89 beta.

This is a development reference for Other Lives. It describes functionality in original words, not reusable dialogue, artwork or code. It is a broad inventory of publicly documented features, not a claim to have triggered every random event or audited the source code. Numerical realism and historical/legal accuracy claimed by the developer have not been independently validated here.

## Evidence and scope

- **Official game:** https://1in8b.life/en/ — launch settings, in-game controls, identity record, status explanations, family display, spending menu, country statistics, life map and survival curve inspected directly.
- **Official About/FAQ:** https://1in8b.life/en/about/ — simulation scope, mortality model, eras, pacing, local saves and optional keepsakes.
- **Official Update notes:** open **Update notes → Show earlier notes** on the official game. Read the available English history from v0.4.41 through v0.7.89. Version references below identify the entries behind less visible routes. These are developer-documented features; they were not all replayed individually.
- **Direct play observation:** a Zambia life through childhood into early adulthood confirmed changing school stages, a rare childhood gift, hobby levels, individual parent ages and closeness, birth-registration consequences, age-specific spending, disabled unaffordable purchases, early-marriage pressure and a choice to request continued education. The adult spending sheet exposed work search, dream-work preparation, child planning, divorce, vocational fields, night/distance university, moving and travel companions. One playthrough cannot establish event frequencies.
- The user’s supplied Kenya journal provides additional examples of childcare, bereavement, medical borrowing, pet treatment, relationship changes and debt follow-through. It is useful experience evidence, not proof of the current version’s complete feature set.

The official FAQ expressly identifies **1in8b.life** as its only official address. Similarly named sites were excluded. Current update notes supersede older notes when an interval or rule changed. The notes document many additions and fixes, but do not publish a complete list of hidden event conditions.

## Main finding for Other Lives

The depth comes from routes that retain state across years. An intention leads to preparation, eligibility, a decision, an uncertain result and later consequences. A refusal, lost income, move, diagnosis or change in household can alter that route. The ordinary years continue to reflect it.

For example, choosing a subject should connect to who pays, study location, work hours, graduation, relevant applications and later earnings. Adding unrelated university memories would not reproduce that continuity. The same principle applies to marriage, migration, business ownership and care.

## Systems that make those routes believable

1. **Eligibility before scheduling.** Age, life stage, current country, year/era, schooling, job, licence, citizenship, available cash, family structure and active commitments exclude unsuitable branches. The notes explicitly correct home events during military service, ordinary purchases in prison, and inaccessible possessions appearing in a story.
2. **Revalidation when a queued event opens.** A divorce invalidates an old pregnancy plan; a death prevents later calls from that person; a move changes the people actually at home. Existing plans cannot simply survive every major change.
3. **Specific people persist.** Individual closeness changes who helps, how a marriage crisis goes and whether a stepchild remains in contact. Family members have their own ages, work, residence, marriages and children.
4. **Money has provenance and timing.** Gift, tuition, mortgage principal, interest, rent, investment, equity and inheritance should not collapse into one unexplained balance. Enough wealth can open a route; insufficient liquidity can close it.
5. **Preparation has downstream value.** Practice can lead to trials/auditions; subjects guide work; years in a profession guide promotions, practice ownership and pension. The benefit need not be immediate or certain.
6. **The four factors change probabilities.** The official status explanation connects health to mortality/work, happiness to circumstances, knowledge to admission/jobs/pay, and relationships to social outcomes. High values improve chances; they do not guarantee an outcome. The developer’s calibration claims require separate validation.
7. **Context is more than food and holidays.** Documented context includes school systems, faith and ritual, employment sectors, housing, medical access, discrimination, legal constraints, local ecology, disaster exposure and historical disruption.
8. **Rare paths retain realistic rarity.** Academy success, public office, professional sport and entertainment do not become ordinary just because the player clicked the relevant option. Failures leave a readable explanation and another possible future.

## Agency without repeated annual chores

The current reference game’s documented approach provides a practical answer to the user’s concern:

| Mechanism | Behaviour documented in the reference | Relevant notes |
|---|---|---|
| Persistent habits | Diet, checkups and ongoing allocation of savings remain set. | 0.5.99, 0.7.85 |
| Optional automatic actions | Courses, job search, hobby lessons, parent support, meals/gifts, donations, trips and tutoring can recur without reopening the same menu manually. | 0.5.78, 0.5.92, 0.5.96, 0.6.30 |
| Affordability gates | Recurring actions pause or do not run when the necessary savings are absent. Some settings expire when their life stage ends. | 0.5.92, 0.6.31, 0.7.36 |
| Remember consequential preferences | Childcare and later children’s university/wedding funding follow earlier choices, with an option to decide separately. | 0.5.74, 0.6.13, 0.6.91 |
| Reopen when circumstances change | A previous funding decision can be reconsidered if it no longer fits savings, or help becomes affordable again. | 0.5.74, 0.7.4 |
| Decline cooldowns | Refused purchases/pets/immigration offers wait longer; repeated refusals can stop unsolicited prompts while manual access remains. | 0.4.48, 0.4.86, 0.7.70 |
| Curated action sheet | A small set of contextually relevant actions appears first; more actions and persistent preferences are secondary. | 0.5.74 |
| Optional automation policy | After several completed lives, preference axes guide automatic choices without overriding affordability or eligibility. | 0.7.86 |

For Other Lives, keep a small persistent set of intentions and routines. Show a decision when the situation changes or commitment becomes meaningful. Do not show a quiet-day card every year merely because a timer elapsed.

## Interface, presentation and replay features

- Births weighted by annual births or population across 172 countries/territories; selectable era; optional comparison to the player's real birthplace.
- Birth record and passport-like identity view: actual current schooling, place, faith, temperament and occasional gift; family income quintile.
- Four visible factors, cash/net worth, earnings, possessions, debt and a more detailed annual money breakdown.
- Static pixel scene showing current home, household and activity/work; household absence/relocation, housing size and day/night presentation can change it. No movement controls are needed to explain the life. The official notes do **not** establish Pokémon Emerald as the source game's art direction; that remains the user's separate requirement for original Other Lives assets.
- School/student/adult/later-life focus options, pause/resume, multiple speeds, next decision and auto decisions; decision cards can be set aside and reopened.
- Full journal, expandable earlier entries, bookmarks, decade navigation, actual stat changes and contextual sourced facts.
- Four parallel same-second lives, yearly life-map tiles and a country/sex survival curve alongside a world comparison.
- Reflection pauses at selected ages; death record, flashbacks, family/children summaries, income/wealth charts, work/investment history and legacy.
- Recent life archive, local browser saves and explicit transfer/import rather than automatic cloud continuity.
- Device-aware light/dark mode, music, mobile controls, heavy-theme filtering and classroom mode.
- Shared life cards, memorials and optional cosmetic keepsakes/film/album/record-book outputs. These are secondary to the simulation work requested here.

## Other Lives: current gaps and implementation order

This is an assessment of the existing code, not a claim of parity. Other Lives has conditional moments, named relationships, degree subjects, family finances, housing/credit actions, job search, persistent routines, skills, source explanations and static household art. A menu entry or a generic card does not by itself count as a complete route.

| Priority | Work needed | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make existing education/funding/work commitments consistent before adding more rare careers. | The displayed course/year, fees, payer, work hours and qualification agree; no unexplained tuition loan or simultaneous incompatible commitments. |
| 2 | Replace isolated major-choice cards with persistent preparation/result/follow-up threads. | The chosen intention changes later eligibility, journal, money, household and normal memories; cancellations invalidate queued cards. |
| 3 | Deepen partnership/parenting/care. | Dating vs cohabiting vs marriage; individual closeness, planning a child, childcare and household income, school support, care and separation follow-through. |
| 4 | Connect career, family enterprise and business operations. | Relevant experience has measurable value; financing is feasible; business failure ends its income and opens a coherent next step. |
| 5 | Make relocation a household and livelihood transition. | Place, job eligibility, school, housing, travel/contact and family co-residence change together; later settlement has an explicit state. |
| 6 | Add retirement, diagnosis/recovery and legacy as full later-life routes. | Pension/runway and reduced work are understandable; care and household effects persist; death record remembers the actual life. |
| 7 | Add carefully gated uncommon routes. | Academics, politics, military, religious vocation, sport, performance and esports have distinct prerequisites, duration, failure and exit paths. |

### Reusable route contract for future development

Each major route should specify: **entry conditions; preparation time; participants; upfront and recurring costs; payer and debt source; modelled chance; mutually exclusive commitments; success/failure effects; follow-up schedule; cancellation rules; persistent memory; passport/scene changes; cooldown and exit choices.**

Every event should recheck the life at the moment it runs. Each factual explanation should define unfamiliar local terms, identify place/time/source, and distinguish measured population context from fictional personal outcomes.

### Remaining uncertainty

The public notes do not establish the exact number of scenarios, every menu in every jurisdiction, hidden probability equations, complete dialogue trees, or implementation quality of each documented fix. This audit should guide original implementation and focused playtesting; it should not be presented as an exhaustive source-code extraction or validation of every demographic/legal claim.
