# Research, features and limitations

**This is an independent game inspired by the broad idea and user experience of a text-first life simulator, not a reproduction of proprietary code, graphics, text or databases.**

## Sources consulted

- [1 in 8 Billion — official site](https://1in8b.life/en/) — manual, gameplay, yearly focus, time, money, people, life record, life map, mortality and source descriptions.
- [UN World Population Prospects](https://population.un.org/wpp/) — potential source of future calibrated country/age/sex life tables and birth weights.
- [World Bank data](https://data.worldbank.org/) — economic and demographic data.
- [UNICEF — Ghana](https://data.unicef.org/country/gha/) — 16% of women ages 20–24 married/in union before age 18 (based on household survey data; not a simulation probability).
- [Ghana Statistical Service](https://census2020.statsghana.gov.gh/) — 2021 census reported 56.7% urban share.
- [UNICEF Bangladesh country data](https://www.unicef.org/bangladesh/en/data-situation-children-bangladesh) — child marriage measures.
- [UNICEF Nigeria](https://www.unicef.org/nigeria/press-releases/unicef-and-partners-find-high-economic-returns-investing-adolescent-girls-northern) — child marriage measures.
- [UNICEF India](https://www.unicef.org/media/172896/file/India.pdf.pdf) — child marriage profile.
- [UNICEF Ethiopia](https://www.unicef.org/ethiopia/press-releases/ethiopia-advances-efforts-end-child-marriage) — child marriage update.
- [UK Department for Education apprenticeships 2024–25](https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/apprenticeships/2024-25).
- [HK Census and Statistics Department](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/page_8000.html) — housing / census context.

## Implemented in 2.4

- Automatic time, adjustable speed, next decision, pause and decision modal
- Set-aside decision and persistent unanswered choices
- 2–5 narrative highlights in most years, including ordinary events, occasional choices and later emotional reflections
- Yearly activity focus linked to age, scenario and effect on state
- Player profile, named relationships, parents differentiated, finances, jobs and life archive
- Limited named job offers aligned to interest and qualifications
- Regional event stories for seven selected countries/regions and source-linked fact bubbles for their selected issues
- Potentially uncomfortable decisions; negative/positive journal styling
- Pixel-art background assets and iPhone PWA icon

## Not yet implemented or not validated

- Country-level birth weighting and age/sex mortality calibrated to current official tables across 172+ countries
- Per-country earnings/wealth distributions, wage inflation, inflation differences, access to vaccines, migration, welfare and family structures calibrated to verified sources
- Historical/future era settings, contemporaries, full survival curves and rich life-map statistics
- A source-backed large-scale context/event library across all countries
- Detailed per-person location transitions, household fiscal modelling and dependents based on verified data
- Independent auditing of macro outcomes against population-level reference distributions

**Warning:** all life outcomes are fictional illustrations. Data sources in fact bubbles describe real populations and must not be interpreted as the chance that the illustrated character will experience a given event.
