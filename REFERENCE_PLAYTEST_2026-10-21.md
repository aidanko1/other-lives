# Direct official-game play observations

Session: 20 October 2026 UTC / 21 October Hong Kong. Official https://1in8b.life/en/, v0.7.89 beta. This is direct interaction with rendered controls/records, distinct from earlier retrieved documentation and limited automated observation. No reference source, hidden state, random seed or assets were inspected/copied into the implementation. These are randomly generated fictional people. No feedback/reflection/public post or purchase was submitted.

## Setup and infant ending
The setup offers about 30/60/90-minute lives (default 60), world/era settings and birth/population draws. I selected 30 minutes, began and used 16×/Next. A boy in middle-income Erbil,Iraq had a birth record with parents/siblings and circumstances. He died in infancy. The actual ending showed cause, summary, survival comparison, parallel lives, local record/rebirth and optional farewell/sharing. Record/rebirth started a different random life.

## Childhood and education
The second birth was a boy in poor rural Punjab,India: parents26/32, older brother4, family occupations/home/social context and four parallel births. At 2 a temperament choice appeared. Setting it aside retained the pending choice and stopped time; household inspection/reopening preserved it. A sympathetic response was recorded. An optional reflection at5 could be privately skipped.
Primary school began automatically at 6 in this life. At 7 a hobby choice established cricket; optional spending showed money and unaffordable items while time stood still. At 12 further school/work choices appeared; at 15 high school/vocational/work followed another optional reflection. Ordinary yearly records also described grades, home work and hardship without making every change a decision.
At 18, uncertain university admission, work, military academy and unaffordable overseas study were offered. Admission succeeded and residence moved to Hyderabad. At 19, subjects included education, business, nursing, engineering and art; engineering was chosen. At 22 a degree completed, followed by work/graduate-study and named salary offers. Software work established the quoted role/pay.

## Adult agency and consequences
A delayed proposed match at 19 fell through. At23 marriage failed despite choosing it: the other person would not proceed against family opposition. A later marriage succeeded at 28. At 30 a fertility intention failed that year; a daughter was later recorded. Childcare at 32 offered own/spouse work breaks, relatives or paid care. Choosing an own break stopped employment. By 47, debt pressure prompted return-to-work and qualified offers referred to the earlier software role/experience. Choices influence outcomes without guaranteeing them.
Health fiction involved treatment, expenses and persistent debt/limited lending. Employer offers compared pay and counted experience. Children aged through schools, university and marriage; parental care, guarantees and family support had continuing consequences. These observations establish that optional family/financial depth belongs to the reference; they do not prove that adding more always helps.
A father's death stopped Next with an explanation and no choice. This motivated ENGINEERING_CYCLE_5.md. Normal-speed bereavement behaviour was not measured; extending this stop to other close-family roles is an explicit judgment.
Optional reflections at 5/15/30/50 were directly seen and privately skipped. They refer to circumstances and parallel lives. Other Lives lacks these; they are documented omissions.

## Retirement, old age and completed ending
At 60, retirement offered a quoted pension, a fixed term of contract re-employment, or different work with pension. Retirement was chosen and recorded. Later years displayed pension savings and continuing family costs. Adult children developed professions, partners, households and descendants.
At 74 the fully rendered record contained 47 entries labelled as decisions: a lower-bound count at that age, not a measured final total or target for every life. The browser operation spanning some later events was interrupted; unobserved intermediate inputs are not claimed from attempts alone. Later rendered history and the final record establish only the outcomes described here.
The life ended naturally at 91. The ending sequence and unobstructed record were visually inspected: cause stroke, engineering education, software work/retirement,63 years with a partner,2 children,3 grandchildren and4 great-grandchildren, parental care, wealth, hobbies and a happy-year highlight. An optional public-sharing sheet was closed without posting. Free/paid memorial/archive options were visible; purchases, paid behaviour and cross-device storage were not tested. Rebirth was verified after the earlier infant ending; another adult life was not started from this final card.

## Limits
This completes one birth→childhood→education/work/family→retirement→old age→death observation plus infant death/rebirth. It is not a distribution study. All countries/eras, numerical risks, economic/medical/legal claims, hidden causes and normal-speed session duration remain unverified. Displayed national statistics were not independently audited and must not become purportedly verified datasets.
No reference artwork/code/substantial prose enters Other Lives. Existing Other Lives school-entry/home-learning, named funding, tabs and static art remain established user preferences. The reference supports the central loop and occasional uncertainty; subjective enjoyment/parity still needs further evaluation.
