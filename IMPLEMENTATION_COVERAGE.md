# Other Lives 6.0: implementation coverage

This inventory maps the public 1 in 8 Billion research to working Other Lives systems. All dialogue, route implementations and code are original. Read the accompanying research audit for evidence and version references. The reference’s full historical, demographic and legal coverage is not reproduced.

| Area | Implemented in this release | Important limits |
|---|---|---|
| Education → work | School-leaving choice before first applications; ten degree-specific graduate offer sets; explicit end/continuation of student work; old-save graduation repair; supervised medical entry and two-year training review | Entry roles and training are game assumptions, not every country’s professional registration system |
| Further education (7 routes) | Admission preparation/retake; equivalency; vocational fields; evening/distance degree; master’s; PhD; faculty progression | Country-specific entrance examinations and degree recognition remain incomplete |
| Work & ownership (2 additional routes) | Business research, business type, financing, operations, hiring/investment, sale/closure; family enterprise succession | Operating costs and valuations are simplified; no complete national company/tax model |
| Relationships & family (6 routes) | Ongoing attempts to conceive; fertility/adoption alternatives; adoption assessment; recurring childcare with reduced parental hours; separation and child residence/support; ageing-parent care | Pregnancy complications, twins, jurisdiction-specific divorce/property law, and later birth-family searches are not fully modeled |
| Home & migration (5 routes) | Destination selection; work offer before moving; overseas subject, admission, funding and completion; residence review; citizenship review; return home | Permit chances and residence intervals are explicit game assumptions, not researched legal eligibility. No comprehensive refugee or historical displacement system |
| Uncommon paths (13 routes) | Sport, music, dance, visual arts, acting/directing, esports, Korean agency training, chess/Go; seafaring; voluntary service/academy; politics; religious vocation | Country-specific service obligations, political terms and clergy rules are incomplete; no astronaut branch; foreign Korean agency relocation is not implemented |
| Health & later life (4 routes) | Symptoms before assessment; treatment and uncertain recovery reviews; emotional/practical support; retirement with earnings history; wishes/legacy | No complete longitudinal disease registry, congenital disability/support model, addiction system, homelessness, prison or care-home simulation |
| Money & property (3 routes) | Investment allocation; cash-equity distinction; sell/downsize/let an owned home; mortgage preservation; family gifts | No reverse mortgage, full rental vacancy/agent system, investment taxes or comprehensive inheritance law |
| Ongoing agency | Routines; optional checkups, insurance, food, hobby practice, gifts and faith practice; paid preferences pause when unaffordable | These are simplified policies. Insurance treatment reduction and death benefit use model assumptions |
| Four factors | Diminishing high-level gains; ongoing maintenance and age/resources/contact pressures; stress/confidence affect visible factors; actual delta logging and spending previews | Balancing parameters require continued playtesting; factor targets are not measured wellbeing datasets |
| Narrative | 242 conditional moment definitions plus route and degree content; actual subject checks; ten-year template cooldown across all people; legacy cooldown migration; pet histories; explicit money labels | Broader country and household-specific writing remains ongoing; old prose cannot always identify the original person or missing bill cause |
| Presentation / continuity | Existing passport, skill levels, named school stages/courses, family quintile circles, device theme, aspect-preserving original household art, GBP default, export/import, archive and replay remain | Complete era-specific architecture and every culture’s visual assets are not present |
| Demographic foundations | Existing 129-country/region model and sourced child-mortality anchors | Adult survival remains fitted, not measured age/sex life tables. The reference’s 172-country historical/future tables and causes are not reproduced |
| Archive / paid services | Read-only archive audit CLI; local export/import and replay | Public memorials, payments, decorated memorial rooms and public community feeds are not implemented |

The 39 routes are staged game contracts with eligibility, price, follow-up and an exit, rather than single generic cards. Automated checks force both successful and unsuccessful branches through their stage contracts and test the reported Medicine playthrough separately. A first pass through every route is not an exhaustive evaluation of every combination of branches, money states or national conditions.
