# Property identity and conserved debt settlement

Base:85deb8d. This source is experimental until its exact-revision gates pass. No production deployment or new player controls.

## Acceptance and implementation
Existing let/move/buy/reoccupy/sell/downsize choices must preserve every asset and secured loan. Owning a rental is distinct from owning the current residence. Each dwelling has a persistent identity, value, use and its own loan; all secured payments enter the budget and all principal repayments reduce the corresponding balance once per age. Sale settles only that property's loan; moving cannot erase another liability. Aggregate values and rental income must reconcile with the records. engine/property.js owns these transitions; existing spending/housing/migration callers use it. engine/finance.js owns all repayments and revaluation; engine/state.js enforces identities, totals, timelines and debt components.

Save schema2 adds the ledger. Older aggregate holdings remain explicitly legacyAggregate records with unknown acquisition age/country, without inventing dwelling counts or historical loans. Migration preserves value, liabilities and journal; current schema2 loading remains an exact clone. A negative legacy family asset balance becomes an explicitly recorded equivalent household liability, conserving household net wealth. Earlier structural repair already converted such negatives; this stage makes the repair traceable before adapters. A 13-record private archive was rechecked read-only:13 passed migration/history/financial consistency/text rendering, with the avatar rasterizer stubbed in Node; one conserved negative-asset repair is recorded. Original bytes unchanged, private records never committed.
